# Stage 1 Product and Information Architecture Design

## Goal

Define the routes, audience journeys, complexity behavior, metric research surface, evidence states, analytics privacy, and measurable gates that the platform implementation consumes.

## Design

The canonical Comparison Lab remains query-driven at `/compare`, while evidence and common comparison routes receive stable server-rendered paths. Complexity is presentation state with URL-first precedence. The metric register records research and publication status without asserting unsupported coverage. Release decisions use technical, evidence, accessibility, performance, and editorial gates.

## Acceptance

- Every planned public surface has a route owner and rendering default.
- All five priority audiences have a complete Comparison Lab journey.
- Complexity precedence and state-preservation behavior are unambiguous.
- Every specification metric appears in the coverage register.
- Missing, mismatch, stale, disputed, and correction states have approved behavior.
- Analytics has an explicit property allowlist and forbidden data.
- Release success is measurable without relying on pageviews.

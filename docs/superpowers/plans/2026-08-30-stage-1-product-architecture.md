**Goal:** Produce the decision-complete product contracts required by the platform scaffold and Comparison Lab.

## Global Constraints

- Complexity changes presentation, never evidence.
- Unreviewed evidence cannot be represented as supported.
- Mobile and accessibility requirements are release gates.
- Analytics cannot collect unnecessary personal data.

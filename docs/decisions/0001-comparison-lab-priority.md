# ADR 0001: Comparison Lab Delivery Priority

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-30

## Context

`ROADMAP.md` places Nuclear 101 before the Evidence System and Energy Comparison Lab. The approved kickoff instead makes the evidence foundation and Comparison Lab the immediate product objective.

## Decision

Build the shared platform, evidence system, and Comparison Lab before Nuclear 101. Release Comparison Lab categories incrementally after the shared platform is complete. Resume the broader roadmap with Nuclear 101 after Comparison Lab V1.

## Consequences

- The Comparison Lab becomes the first public flagship.
- Nuclear 101 remains required but moves to the first post-flagship stage.
- Shared complexity and evidence primitives must be reusable by later lessons.
